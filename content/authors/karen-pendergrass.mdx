Karen Pendergrass is the founder of Heavy Metal Facts and the Paleo Foundation. Her work focuses on translating complex contaminant science into accessible, evidence-first reporting for consumers, brands, and regulators. She specializes in heavy metal exposure pathways, food safety certification, and standards-minded journalism.
